# Singalong AI: Architectural Review & Roadmap Request
1. ##Project Context & Vision
I am building an automated + human-in-the-loop CLI and web tool (`singalong`) designed to turn music tracks (primarily Chinese gacha/game OSTs like *Wuthering Waves*, *Genshin Impact*, *Honkai: Star Rail*, etc., and anime music) into professional sing-along / karaoke videos for YouTube.
The target output is a 1080p/4K 60fps video with:
- Syllable-by-syllable, progressive left-to-right color-fill wiping (`\kf`).
- 3-tiered synchronized subtitles stacked vertically:
1. US-English phonetic respelling (for non-Chinese speakers).
  2. Pinyin with proper tone marks.
  3. Official Chinese characters (Hanzi).
- Automatic handling of call-and-response vocals (lead singer centered, backing choir positioned off to the side with distinct color/italics).
- Output rendered on either a clean black canvas or burned directly over gameplay/scenic video backgrounds.
Hardware Environment: Local Linux machine with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 (24GB VRAM) and system FFmpeg with `libass` and `nvenc`.
---
## 2. What Is Currently Built & Working
### Pipeline 1: Extraction & Alignment (`singalong.py`)
1. **Audio Download**: `yt-dlp` extracts high-quality audio (`.wav`) from a YouTube URL.
2. **Vocal Stem Separation**: `demucs` (`htdemucs`) isolates `vocals.wav`.
3. **Official Lyrics Fetching**: Queries NetEase Cloud Music's public unauthenticated API (`cloudsearch` + `lyric` endpoint) to obtain 100% studio-official Hanzi lyrics with synchronized `.lrc` line timestamps.
4. **Acoustic Stopwatch via FunASR**: FunASR's `paraformer-zh` model runs on CUDA to produce acoustic token timestamps.
5. **Hybrid Marrying & Hallucination Dropping**:
   - Rather than letting ASR guess words (which causes homophone typos like 云深林 instead of 玄翎), or relying only on line-level LRC timestamps (which lack syllable timings and breath pauses):
   - NetEase provides the ground-truth text and line boundaries, while Paraformer acts as the acoustic stopwatch.
   - Intro noise hallucinations (e.g. `对的对的好的` at 0.01s) are dropped by bounding tokens to the first LRC line.
   - Mid-line breath pauses (e.g. 3-second pause between `玄翎飞` and `飞过江`) are preserved by clustering acoustic tokens across silence gaps.
6. **Call-and-Response Parsing**:
   - Parentheticals `（...）` in NetEase lyrics are parsed to separate lead vocals from choir responses, flagging `"is_backing": true/false` in the JSON.
7. **Phonetics Generation**: `pypinyin` produces tone-marked Pinyin, and a phonetic respelling engine generates US-English approximations (e.g. `shywen-leeng-fay`).
8. **Outputs**: Produces `<video_id>.json` (millisecond per-token timings) and `<video_id>.txt` (human inspection preview).
### Pipeline 2: Parameterized Video Renderer (`colorfill.py`)
1. **Configuration**: Driven by `config.json` (typography, font size, stroke outline, colors for unfilled/filled text, margins, and placement).
2. **Backing Placement**: Automatically places lead vocals at `bottom-center` in Gold (`#FFD700`) and backing vocals at `bottom-right` in Cyan (`#70D6FF`), scaled and italicized.
3. **Hardware Acceleration**: Generates an Advanced SubStation Alpha (`.ass`) file with `\kf` karaoke wipe tags and burns it into video via FFmpeg using `libass` and `h264_nvenc` on the RTX 4090 (~480 fps render speed).

## 1. Critical Architectural Assessment

### A. The "Count Mismatch" Problem in Hybrid Marrying

Using `paraformer-zh` purely as an acoustic stopwatch works well when the number of emitted acoustic tokens in a window equals the number of Hanzi characters in the NetEase line ($N_{\text{ASR}} = N_{\text{Hanzi}}$). However, singing introduces two major failure modes:

* **Melisma (Vowel Extension):** A singer holds one character (e.g., `啊` or `飞`) across three pitch changes. Paraformer often emits 2–3 tokens (or repeats vowel tokens) for a single character, causing $N_{\text{ASR}}$ to be greater than $N_{\text{Hanzi}}$ and shifting subsequent syllable timings rightward.
* **Elision & Fast Rap:** In rapid passages, Paraformer may merge two adjacent syllables with similar formants into a single acoustic token ($N_{\text{ASR}}$ less than $N_{\text{Hanzi}}$).
* **The Fix:** Replace index-based token marrying with **Phoneme-Level Dynamic Time Warping (DTW)** or **CTC Forced Alignment**. Convert both the NetEase ground-truth Hanzi and the Paraformer output into Pinyin initials/finals, run a Needleman-Wunsch alignment or CTC trellis within the LRC line boundaries, and map the acoustic timestamps back to the ground-truth characters.

### B. `libass` Vertical Stacking & Horizontal Drift

In standard `.ass` subtitles, a `\kf` tag sweeps across a text line at a constant pixel-per-centisecond rate. If you stack three lines (US Phonetics, Pinyin, Hanzi) as three separate `Dialogue:` events with identical `\kf` durations, **their visual wipe heads will desynchronize horizontally**.

* **Why:** `玄` (1 character, ~45px wide) vs. `xuán` (4 characters, ~70px wide) vs. `shywen` (6 characters, ~105px wide). While each individual syllable finishes at the exact same millisecond, the *overall width* of the three lines differs, so the vertical alignment of each syllable's wipe point shifts across the screen.
* **The Fix:** Instead of rendering three long lines, calculate the bounding-box width of the widest tier (usually US Phonetics) for each syllable, and use **per-syllable `\pos(x,y)` absolute positioning** or invisible padding spaces (`\h`) so that every syllable forms a strict vertical column (a "Ruby text" grid).

### C. Polyphonic Characters (多音字) in `pypinyin`

`pypinyin` defaults to modern standard speech frequencies, which frequently mispronounces classical or poetic Chinese lyrics common in *Wuthering Waves* and *Honkai: Star Rail* OSTs:

* **Classical/Lyrical Readings:** `长安` (`cháng` vs `zhǎng`), `叶` (`yè` vs `xié` in poetic rhymes), `行` (`xíng` vs `háng`), `血` (`xuè` vs `xiě`), or `骑` (`qí` vs `jì`).
* **Tone Sandhi:** Decide early whether your Pinyin tier displays **lexical tones** (`bù shì`, `yī gè`) or **sung/sandhi tones** (`bú shì`, `yí gè`). Standard practice for learners and singers is lexical tone marks on Pinyin, but phonetic respelling reflecting actual pronunciation.

### D. NVENC Chroma Subsampling on Colored Text

Burning Cyan (`#70D6FF`) and Gold (`#FFD700`) text with thin strokes onto a dark background using standard `h264_nvenc` (`yuv420p`) causes noticeable **4:2:0 chroma bleed**, making thin phonetic text look blurry or pixelated on YouTube.

* **The Fix:** Ensure `libass` renders in full RGB or `yuv444p` before the final scale, and add a minimum `2.5px–3px` hard black outline (`\bord3\shad0`) so high-contrast luminance edges protect the color fill from chroma subsampling artifacts.

Before finalizing the schemas for the linter and building the web editor, consider these five architectural decisions:

1. **Multi-Tier Ruby Alignment Strategy:** In `colorfill.py`, are you currently emitting 3 separate `Dialogue:` lines per phrase with standard `\kf` tags, or are you already calculating column widths so each Hanzi sits directly underneath its Pinyin and English phonetic equivalent?
2. **Simultaneous vs. Sequential Call-and-Response:** In NetEase `.lrc` files, parenthetical backing vocals `（...）` are placed inline at the end of the text string, even when the choir sings *simultaneously* over the lead singer's held final note. ## 3. Pre-Render Linter Specification (`lint.py`)

Run these five mathematical and heuristic checks on `<video_id>.json` immediately after Pipeline 1 finishes. Each token $i$ in line $L$ has start time $t_{i,\text{start}}$, end time $t_{i,\text{end}}$, and duration $d_i = t_{i,\text{end}} - t_{i,\text{start}}$.

| Rule ID | Metric / Formula | Threshold | What It Catches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **`ERR_COUNT_MISMATCH`** | $\Vert{}N_{\text{Hanzi}} - N_{\text{ASR\_tokens}}\Vert{} > 0$ | Exact mismatch | Melisma splitting a syllable into 2 tokens, or rapid articulation dropping a syllable. |
| **`WARN_MICRO_SYLLABLE`** | $d_i < 60\text{ ms}$ | Less than $60\text{ ms}$ | Forced-alignment squish where 3+ characters got crammed into a tiny acoustic transition. |
| **`WARN_RUNAWAY_HOLD`** | $d_i > 2500\text{ ms}$ AND $\text{RMS}(t_{i,\text{start}} + 800\text{ms} \dots t_{i,\text{end}}) < -42\text{ dBFS}$ | Greater than $2.5\text{ s}$ with low tail energy | Unbounded final token stretching across an instrumental break until the next LRC line starts. |
| **`WARN_UNIFORM_FALLBACK`** | $\sigma(d_1, d_2, \dots, d_n) < 5\text{ ms}$ for $n \ge 4$ | StdDev less than $5\text{ ms}$ | Alignment failure where the code fell back to dividing the line duration equally ($D_{\text{line}} / n$). |
| **`WARN_SILENCE_GHOST`** | $\text{MeanRMS}_{\text{vocals.wav}}(t_{i,\text{start}}, t_{i,\text{end}}) < -45\text{ dBFS}$ | Less than $-45\text{ dBFS}$ | Token placed inside an instrumental breath pause or hallucinated on reverb tail. |
| **`ERR_LINE_BOUNDARY_BLEED`** | $t_{n,\text{end}}(L_k) > t_{1,\text{start}}(L_{k+1})$ (same vocal track) | Overlap greater than $0\text{ ms}$ | Lead vocal line overlapping the next lead vocal line, causing `libass` vertical collision jumping. |

### Composite Line Confidence Score

Assign every line a confidence score $C_L \in [0, 100]$ so the web editor can sort or highlight problem lines in red/amber:

$$C_L = 100 - \left( 40 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{\text{mismatch}} + 15 \cdot N_{\text{micro}} + 20 \cdot N_{\text{ghost}} + 25 \cdot \mathbb{I}_{\text{uniform}} \right)$$

Any line with $C_L < 80$ is automatically flagged in the CLI output and added to a "Jump to Next Issue" queue in the web editor.

## 4. Web Editor Technical Blueprint (`editor.py`)

To hit your goal of fixing a song in **1–2 minutes**, mouse dragging alone is too slow. The editor should be built around **keyboard-driven rhythm tapping** and **dual-stem waveform scrubbing**.

### Recommended Stack

* **Backend:** **FastAPI** (`uvicorn`) inside `editor.py`. Supports `Range` HTTP headers out of the box (mandatory for instant HTML5 audio seeking without waiting for full `.wav` buffered loads) and Server-Sent Events (SSE) to stream FFmpeg render progress back to the browser.
* **Frontend Reactivity:** **SolidJS** (or Vanilla JS with fine-grained signals). Why: When scrubbing audio at 60fps, fine-grained reactivity updates the CSS `--wipe-progress` variable on the active syllable DOM node directly without triggering virtual DOM diffing across hundreds of lyric tokens.
* **Waveform Engine:** **`wavesurfer.js` (v7)** with the **Regions Plugin**.
* Load `vocals.wav` as the visual waveform (so syllable onsets and breath gaps are visually obvious as spikes), while syncing playback with `original_mix.wav` via a quick mute/solo toggle (`V` key).
* Render each syllable as a colored Wavesurfer Region on the timeline. Dragging a region boundary immediately mutates the syllable's start/end ms.



### High-Speed UX Workflows (The 1-Minute Fix)

1. **Live CSS `\kf` Preview:**
Render the 3-tier lyrics above the waveform using the exact fonts from `config.json`. Bind each syllable's fill state to `background: linear-gradient(90deg, #FFD700 var(--fill), #FFFFFF var(--fill))` with `-webkit-background-clip: text`. You see the exact karaoke wipe in real time without running FFmpeg.
2. **"Tap-Along" Re-Timing Mode (`T` Key):**
When a fast line is drifted, select the line, press `T`, and the audio plays at `0.75x` speed from 2 seconds before the line. Tap the **`Spacebar`** on the onset of each syllable. Your keypress timestamps overwrite `start_ms` for each token sequentially, using acoustic energy drop-offs to auto-cap the final token.
3. **One-Click Modifiers:**
* **`Alt + Click` on a syllable:** Toggles `"is_deleted": true` (renders with red strikethrough; omitted from `.ass` generation).
* **`B` Key (with text/region selected):** Toggles `"is_backing": true` on the selected tokens, immediately moving them to the Cyan backing track lane in the preview.
* **`Double-Click` on Pinyin/Phonetics:** Opens an inline `<input>` to override a polyphonic reading without touching timings.



### Backend API Endpoints (`editor.py`)

* `GET /api/project/<video_id>` — Returns `<video_id>.json`, `config.json`, and linter warnings.
* `GET /api/audio/<video_id>/<stem>` — Streams `original.wav` or `vocals.wav` with `Accept-Ranges: bytes`.
* `POST /api/project/<video_id>/save` — Atomically writes updated JSON (creating a `.bak` snapshot first) and re-runs the linter.
* `POST /api/project/<video_id>/render` — Spawns `colorfill.py` + `ffmpeg` subprocess and streams FPS/progress via SSE.

## 5. Solving Problem C & Problem D

### Problem C: Dual-Stem Ingestion (Lead vs. Backing Vocals)

Using `audio-separator` (which wraps UVR MDX-Net and Roformer models in Python) on your RTX 4090 takes ~8–12 seconds per song and dramatically improves alignment accuracy on choral tracks:

1. **Stage 1 (Vocal Isolation):** Run `Kim_Vocal_2` or `BS-Roformer-Viperx-1297` on the raw mix to extract a cleaner `vocals_all.wav` than `htdemucs`.
2. **Stage 2 (Karaoke/Backing Split):** Run `mel_band_roformer_karaoke_aufr33_viperx` on `vocals_all.wav`. This yields:
* `lead_vocals.wav` (dry center lead)
* `backing_vocals.wav` (harmonies, choir, call-and-response)


3. **Dual-Track FunASR Alignment:**
* Parse the NetEase line first: strip parentheticals `（...）` into a `backing_text` buffer and keep the main text in `lead_text`.
* Run FunASR alignment for `lead_text` against **`lead_vocals.wav`**. Because the choir is stripped out, Paraformer won't prematurely trigger or stretch lead tokens over backing vocals.
* Run FunASR alignment for `backing_text` against **`backing_vocals.wav`** within the window $[t_{\text{line\_start}}, t_{\text{next\_line\_start}} + 1500\text{ms}]$. This allows backing vocals to overlap naturally with the lead singer's held notes!



### Problem D: Non-NetEase Fallbacks & Generalization

Build a **4-Tier Waterfall Resolver** for lyrics acquisition:

1. **Tier 1: Multi-Provider LRC Search:** If NetEase misses, query **QQ Music API** (`y.qq.com`) and **LRCLIB** (`lrclib.net` API). QQ Music actually has native **character-by-character `.qrc` (QRC) XML timestamps** for many Chinese game OSTs, which can serve as ground-truth timing or a high-precision reference.
2. **Tier 2: Unsynchronized Official Hanzi (Genius / HoYoWiki / KuroWiki / Video Description):**
If you have the raw Hanzi text without timestamps:
* Run **WhisperX (`large-v3`)** or **SenseVoice** on `lead_vocals.wav` to get coarse phrase-level segments.
* Use dynamic programming (Levenshtein fuzzy matching in Pinyin space) to anchor your ground-truth Hanzi lines to the ASR segments, then run your existing FunASR token alignment inside each anchored line.


3. **Tier 3: Romanized/Pinyin Only -> Hanzi Reconstruction:**
Avoid translating Pinyin to Hanzi without acoustic context—even Qwen 2.5 14B will guess wrong on poetic homophones if given only Pinyin. Instead, feed a **dual prompt** to a local LLM: pass *both* the Genius Pinyin AND the noisy `paraformer-zh` / `Whisper` Hanzi transcript. The ASR provides the exact acoustic homophone candidates, and the Pinyin locks the phonetic readings.
4. **Tier 4: Zero-Lyric Cold Start:**
Run `Whisper large-v3` (for punctuation and line segmentation) + `paraformer-zh` (for syllable timing), flag the entire file as `"source": "asr_unverified"` so the linter highlights low-probability bigrams for quick human review in `editor.py`.
