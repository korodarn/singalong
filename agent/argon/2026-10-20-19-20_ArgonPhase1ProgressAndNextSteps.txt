## 2. Root-Cause Diagnosis & What Was Implemented

### A. Automated Pre-Render QA Linter (`lint.py`)
We implemented `lint.py` to evaluate `<video_id>.json` against the isolated vocal stem (`work/htdemucs/<vid>/vocals.wav`) using 10ms RMS energy (dBFS) checks:
- **`ERR_LINE_BOUNDARY_BLEED`:** Catches same-track (lead-on-lead or backing-on-backing) temporal overlaps that cause `libass` vertical collision jumping.
- **`WARN_UNIFORM_FALLBACK`:** Catches lines where syllable durations have $\sigma < 5\text{ ms}$ (flat division across the line window).
- **`WARN_MICRO_SYLLABLE`:** Catches syllables squished below $60\text{ ms}$.
- **`WARN_SILENCE_GHOST`:** Catches syllables placed where `vocals.wav` mean RMS is below $-45\text{ dBFS}$ (instrumental breaks or reverb tails).
- **`WARN_RUNAWAY_HOLD`:** Catches syllables held longer than $2500\text{ ms}$ whose tail drops below $-42\text{ dBFS}$.

### B. Acoustic-Energy + Onset-Weighted Alignment Engine (`singalong.py`)
**Why the Korean video was previously off:**
1. `paraformer-zh` emitted Chinese token counts that rarely matched Korean syllable counts ($N_{\text{ASR}} \ne N_{\text{Hangul}}$).
2. Whenever `len(norm_chars) != len(toks)`, the previous `align_clause()` discarded all internal acoustic timestamps and divided `span_end - span_start` into identical uniform steps (`WARN_UNIFORM_FALLBACK` on 37/40 Korean lines and 23/44 Chinese lines).
3. Worse, a single stray ASR token during an instrumental break stretched lines across 15–19 seconds of silence (e.g., `Please tell me, would you love me` at `01:27..01:46` in `XEGWxPmWTYs`, and `待到秋夜凉送我回故乡` at `02:13..02:29` in `jgWBRVy3uIg`).

**How we fixed it in `singalong.py`:**
- **Vocal Stem RMS & Onset Analyzer (`VocalAnalyzer`):** Computes a 10ms-resolution dBFS RMS curve and `librosa` spectral flux onset envelope directly from `vocals.wav`.
- **Active Voiced Islands:** Detects actual singing intervals (`> -41.5 dBFS`, bridging brief consonant closures `< 220ms`) inside each LRC window, trimming leading/trailing instrumental silence so syllables only wipe when the singer's voice is actually present.
- **Phantom LRC Line Detection:** Automatically detected and dropped an unsung copy-pasted chorus line in `XEGWxPmWTYs.netease.lrc` at `[02:57.50] 나를 내버려 두지는 말아줘`, where the studio track drops into a `-72.9 dBFS` instrumental bridge break before `03:00.86`.
- **Non-Uniform Acoustic + Linguistic Weighting:** When $N_{\text{ASR}} \ne N_{\text{Chars}}$, character durations are weighted by interpolated ASR token density, `vocals.wav` onset/RMS energy envelopes, and linguistic syllable structure (diphthongs, batchim codas, word-final lengthening, and phrase-final sustain).
- **Same-Track Non-Overlap Guard (`enforce_non_overlapping_lines`):** Guarantees `0ms` bleed between consecutive lines on the same vocal lane.
- **Local ModelScope Snapshot & ASR Caching:** Resolved sandbox read-only errors by loading cached ModelScope snapshots directly and caching raw ASR timestamps to `work/<vid>.asr.json`.

### C. Korean Surface Phonology Engine (`apply_korean_phonology` in `singalong.py`)
Previously, Hangul characters were decomposed one block at a time in isolation, producing unpronounceable consonant clusters and inaccurate readings. We implemented cross-syllable Korean phonological assimilation before emitting Revised Romanization (`pinyin`) and US-English phonetics (`en`):
- **Liaison (연음법칙):** `내 맘을 내가` $\rightarrow$ `nae ma meul nae ga` (`nay-mah-mool-nay-gah`); `날들을` $\rightarrow$ `nal deu reul` (`nahl-doo-rool`); `슬픔을` $\rightarrow$ `seul peu meul` (`sool-poo-mool`).
- **Coda Neutralization & Nasalization (비음화):** `알 수 없는` $\rightarrow$ `al su eom neun` (`ahl-soo-awm-noon`, instead of `awbs-noon`); `붙잡는` $\rightarrow$ `but jam neun` (`boot-jahm-noon`, instead of `boot-jahp-noon`); `것만` $\rightarrow$ `geon man` (`gawn-mahn`).
- **Aspiration (거센소리되기):** `늦지 않게` $\rightarrow$ `neut ji an ke` (`noot-jee-ahn-keh`, instead of `ahnh-geh`); `지독한` $\rightarrow$ `ji do kan` (`jee-doh-kahn`).
- **Liquidization (유음화):** `알려줘` $\rightarrow$ `al lyeo jwo` (`ahl-lyaw-jwaw`); `떠밀려 가` $\rightarrow$ `tteo mil lyeo ga` (`taw-meel-lyaw-gah`).
- **Punctuation & CJK-Latin Spacing:** Fixed comma-adjacent English words (`Please tell me, would you love me`) and code-switched boundaries (`Please tell me 나를 사랑해줘`) so spaces are preserved cleanly.

### D. Per-Syllable Ruby Column Alignment (`colorfill.py`)
To eliminate the horizontal wipe head drift across the 3 stacked subtitle tiers:
- For multi-tier lines (`Hanzi/Hangul` + `Pinyin/RR` + `English`), `colorfill.py` now computes each token column's bounding width $\max(W_{\text{HZ}}, W_{\text{PY}}, W_{\text{EN}})$ with inter-syllable (`12px`) and inter-word (`26px`) spacing, auto-scaling via `\fscx\fscy` if a long line approaches screen margins.
- Emits synchronized `\an2\pos(center_x, y_tier)\k<wait_cs>\kf<dur_cs>` events so all 3 tiers for syllable $i$ are hardware-locked to the exact same horizontal center coordinate `center_x` and wipe over the exact same centisecond window.
- Pure-English lines (`No one told me why`, `Should I sell my love to someone else`) continue to collapse cleanly into a single centered karaoke line.

## 3. Recommended Next Milestones (Scoped for Modular Sessions)

To keep future sessions focused and within comfortable token budgets, we can tackle the remaining roadmap in self-contained increments:

1. **Milestone A: Zero-Build Web Editor MVP (`editor.py`)**
   - Single-file FastAPI backend (`Accept-Ranges: bytes` audio streaming, JSON load/save, `custom_dict.json` write-back, and `lint.py` issue queue) + embedded SolidJS/Vanilla + `wavesurfer.js` v7 frontend.
   - Spacebar "Tap-Along" rhythm retiming mode (`T` key) and `Alt+Click` syllable deletion.
2. **Milestone B: Japanese (`pykakasi`) & Multi-Mora Kanji Alignment**
   - Add Japanese Hiragana/Katakana/Kanji detection in `singalong.py`.
   - Decompose Kanji compounds into Furigana/Mora units so multi-syllable Kanji (`私` = *wa-ta-shi*, `光` = *hi-ka-ri*) align accurately across multiple acoustic beats.
3. **Milestone C: Dual-Stem Lead/Backing Vocal Split & Waterfall Lyric Resolver**
   - Optional integration of `audio-separator` (`MelBandRoformer-Karaoke`) for tracks with heavy overlapping choirs.
   - Add `LRCLIB` (`lrclib.net`) and QQ Music (`.qrc`) fallback fetchers in `singalong.py` when NetEase Cloud Music misses.
